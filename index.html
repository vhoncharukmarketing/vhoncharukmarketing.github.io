Case 05 · Sushi delivery chain · from the ad account

What drove the results

Remarketing

The key decision. Before the rebuild weekday ROAS jumped around 2–3×. After it, 4–6× on weekdays and a steady result all month.

Formats

UGC and motion video worked best. Instagram traffic ran in parallel as an extra sales channel.

Seasonality

Friday to Sunday are the order peaks. Budget boosts for Black Friday and New Year. Sushi has no dead season.

Approach

No magic tricks: constant testing of audiences, visuals and copy to find the winning combinations, with ROAS as the main KPI.
